1. EMPLOYMENT POLICY
=========================================================

The company is committed to maintaining a professional, inclusive, and ethical work environment for all employees. Employment decisions are made based on qualifications, experience, business requirements, and performance without discrimination based on race, religion, gender, age, disability, nationality, marital status, or any other legally protected characteristic.

Every employee is expected to maintain professionalism, integrity, honesty, confidentiality, and respect while carrying out their responsibilities. Employees must comply with all company rules, policies, and applicable laws.

Employees are responsible for understanding company policies during onboarding and acknowledging that they have read and accepted the employee handbook.

Any violation of company policies may result in counseling, written warning, suspension, or termination depending on the severity of the violation.

2. Job description preparation

5. Technical assessment

4. PROBATION POLICY
=========================================================

All newly hired employees are placed on probation for six months.

The probation period allows managers to evaluate:

• Technical skills
• Communication
• Attendance
• Discipline
• Team collaboration
• Productivity
• Learning ability
• Professional behavior

Managers conduct monthly performance discussions.

At the end of probation, employees may be:

• Confirmed
• Extended for three months
• Terminated due to unsatisfactory performance

=========================================================
5. ATTENDANCE POLICY
=========================================================

Office working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees must record attendance using the company's attendance system.

Late arrival policy:

First two late arrivals in a month are allowed.

Third late arrival may result in a half-day leave deduction.

Continuous late attendance may result in disciplinary action.

Employees who forget to mark attendance must notify HR within 24 hours.

=========================================================
6. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
=========================================================

Employees receive twenty-five days of paid annual leave every calendar year.

Leave Rules

• Apply at least five working days in advance.
• Leave must be approved before travel.
• Leave application should be submitted through the HR Portal.
• Maximum carry forward is ten days.
• Encashment allowed for fifteen unused days.
• Leave balance can be viewed through the HR portal.
• Employees should avoid applying leave during critical project delivery unless approved.

Managers may reject leave requests due to business requirements.

=========================================================
7. SICK LEAVE POLICY
=========================================================

Employees receive twelve paid sick leave days every year.

Employees must:

Submit Form SL-01.

Inform reporting manager immediately.

Upload medical documents when required.

A doctor's certificate is mandatory for more than three consecutive sick leave days.

Unused sick leave expires at the end of every calendar year.

Repeated sick leave patterns may trigger HR wellness discussions.

=========================================================
8. CASUAL LEAVE POLICY
=========================================================

Employees receive six casual leave days annually.

Casual leave may be used for:

Family emergencies

Personal work

Medical appointments

Urgent personal matters

Maximum two consecutive casual leave days may be taken.

Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave.

=========================================================
9. MATERNITY LEAVE POLICY
=========================================================

Eligible female employees receive twenty-six weeks of paid maternity leave.

Eligibility:

Minimum six months of continuous employment.

Employees should notify HR one month before expected delivery.

Required documents:

Doctor certificate

Expected delivery report

Medical records

The company may extend leave by four weeks in case of certified medical complications.

=========================================================
10. PATERNITY LEAVE POLICY
=========================================================

Eligible male employees receive five working days of paid paternity leave.

Leave must be utilized within thirty days of childbirth.

Employees must submit:

Hospital discharge summary
