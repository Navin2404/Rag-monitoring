# Company HR Leave Policy Document
Version: 2.0 | Last Updated: January 2025

## 1. Annual Leave Policy
Employees are eligible for 25 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance.
Leave must be approved by the direct manager before proceeding.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to 10 days to the next year.
Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal under "My Leaves" section.

## 2. Sick Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
To apply for sick leave, employees must fill out Form SL-01 on the HR portal.
If sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days, a doctor's certificate must be submitted.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.
Repeated sick leave patterns may be reviewed by HR for wellness support.

## 3. Casual Leave Policy
Employees are eligible for 6 days of casual leave per year.
Casual leave can be taken for personal emergencies and family events.
Casual leave cannot be clubbed with annual leave.
Maximum 2 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at once.
Casual leave must be applied on the same day or one day before in the HR portal.

## 4. Maternity Leave Policy
Female employees are eligible for 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Maternity leave applies after completing 6 months of employment.
Employees must notify HR at least 1 month before the expected delivery date.
Maternity leave can be extended by 4 weeks in case of medical complications.
To apply, submit Form ML-01 along with the doctor's certificate to HR.

## 5. Paternity Leave Policy
Male employees are eligible for 5 days of paid paternity leave.
Paternity leave must be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.
Employee must submit birth certificate copy to HR after availing leave.
Apply using Form PL-01 on the HR portal at least 1 week in advance.

## 6. Bereavement Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 3 days of paid bereavement leave.
Bereavement leave is applicable for immediate family members only.
Immediate family includes spouse, children, parents, and siblings.
Supporting documents may be required upon return to work.
Additional unpaid leave can be requested if needed.

## 7. Work From Home Policy
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
WFH must be pre-approved by the manager via email or HR portal.
During WFH, employees must be available on official communication channels.
WFH is not applicable during training periods or probation.
Repeated WFH violations may lead to cancellation of WFH privileges.

## 8. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
Employees can apply for leave without pay after exhausting all paid leaves.
LWP requires HR manager approval and department head sign-off.
LWP affects monthly salary calculation proportionately.
More than 30 days of LWP requires management committee approval.
Submit Form LWP-01 to HR with justification letter.

## 9. Public Holidays
The company observes 10 national public holidays per year.
Holiday list is published every December for the following year.
If an employee works on a public holiday, compensatory off will be granted.
Compensatory off must be used within 30 days of the holiday worked.

## 10. Leave Encashment Policy
Employees can encash up to 15 days of unused annual leave per year.
Leave encashment is processed during the December payroll cycle.
Encashment rate is calculated based on basic salary per day.
Submit Form LE-01 to HR before November 30th to avail encashment.
